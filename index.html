Constitution Index

Article I: Identity

The Name of this organization shall be ALPHA RHO SIGMA FRATERNITY AND SORORITY.

Section 1 defines the organization's legal standing as a fraternal student body.

Article III: Objectives

Advancing fraternal assistance, academic scholarship, and the spirit of righteousness.

Every member must be an advocate of the founding principles established in 1965.

Article IV: Membership

Requires recruitment of at least thirteen (13) candidates for a chapter to operate.

Defines the criteria for becoming a "True Rhoan" member.

Article XI: Amendment

Final ratification requires the signature of Founder Atty. Rodrigo G. Roy.

Ensures the legal and organizational continuity of the Fraternity/Sorority.
